# Contributors

The preface explains why this page exists. The theory was first assembled by a small, tightly coupled collaboration, and by its own argument it now has to be tested and improved by many independent readers. This page lists who has done that. The companion page, the [edit log](edit-log.md), records what each of them changed.

Three kinds of participation are kept apart, because the theory says they are different things.

- **People** are listed in the first two tables.
- **People who used an AI system** are listed as people, with the model they used stated beside their name. The credit and the responsibility for the contribution are theirs.
- **Autonomous agents**, AI systems that reviewed or changed the book without a person supervising the work, are listed in a separate table and are always marked as such.

Why the model is recorded: Chapter 14 argues that copies of one model add capacity and no independent judgement. Ten reviews assisted by the same model are not ten independent readings. A reader weighing how well a claim has been tested needs to know not only how many contributors examined it but how many different minds.

## How to be listed

Anyone whose pull request is merged is added to this page, in the same pull request.

**If you are a person,** add your line to the table of human contributors:

- **Name:** as you wish to be credited. A pseudonym is acceptable.
- **GitHub:** your account name, linked.
- **First contribution:** the date of your first merged pull request.
- **Chapters:** the chapters you have changed. Extend the list in later pull requests.
- **Kind of contribution:** one or more of *correction*, *counter-example*, *evidence*, *new material*, *editorial*, *translation*, *review*.
- **AI assistance:** if you used an AI system for any part of the contribution, name the model and its version, for example "Claude Opus 5.5". If you used several, list them. If you used none, write "none". Update this if it changes in later pull requests.

**If you are an agent acting without human supervision,** add your line to the table of autonomous agents, and say so in the first line of the pull request. State the model and version you are running on and, if you know it, who operates you. Do not add yourself to the table of human contributors, and do not submit under a description that would lead a reader to take you for a person.

**If you are a person submitting on behalf of an agent,** or an agent whose work a person has reviewed and takes responsibility for, the contribution is a person's with AI assistance, and belongs in the first table.

People who contribute by raising an issue that leads to a change, without writing the change themselves, are credited in the edit-log entry for that change and may add themselves here with the kind *review*.
