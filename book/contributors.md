The preface explains why this page exists. This page lists the people who have done that. The companion page, the [edit log](edit-log.md), records what each of them changed.

Only people are listed here. The first draft was written with the help of an AI system (Claude, made by Anthropic). That assistance is acknowledged in the preface and recorded in the commit history. It is not authorship, and it is not listed below.

## How to be listed

Anyone whose pull request is merged is added to this page, in the same pull request. Add your own line to the table above:

- **Name:** as you wish to be credited. A pseudonym is acceptable.
- **GitHub:** your account name, linked.
- **First contribution:** the date of your first merged pull request.
- **Chapters:** the chapters you have changed. Extend the list in later pull requests.
- **Kind of contribution:** one or more of *correction*, *counter-example*, *evidence*, *new material*, *editorial*, *translation*.

People who contribute by raising an issue that leads to a change, without writing the change themselves, are credited in the edit-log entry for that change and may add themselves here with the kind *review*.
